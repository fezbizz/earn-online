How to Spot Good Products Online — Buyer's Guide

Buying products online can be risky. How do you know if something actually works before you pay? This guide covers how to spot good products, avoid scams, and use money-back guarantees to your advantage.

The golden rule: If a product is sold through ClickBank, it comes with a 60-day money-back guarantee. That means you can try any product risk-free. If it does not work, you get your money back. This guide helps you avoid wasting time on products that are not worth trying.

1. Check for a money-back guarantee

The single most important thing to look for. A product with a 60-day money-back guarantee is safe to try — you lose nothing if it does not work. If a product has no guarantee, do not buy it. A company that stands behind its product lets you try it risk-free.

2. Read the pros AND cons

Honest reviews list both pros and cons. If a review only lists the good stuff and never mentions any downsides, it is a sales page, not a review. Every product has limitations — a review that admits them is a review you can trust.

3. Watch out for over-the-top claims

"Lose 30 pounds in 3 days!" "Cure your arthritis overnight!" If it sounds too good to be true, it is. Real products make realistic claims like "may help" or "supports" — not "cures" or "eliminates." Be skeptical of anything that promises instant results.

4. Check the ingredient or content list

If a supplement will not tell you what is in it, do not buy it. You have a right to know what you are putting in your body. Same for digital products — if they will not tell you what you actually get, skip it. Transparency matters.

5. Look for independent reviews

Do not rely only on the testimonials on the sales page. Search for the product name on Google and YouTube. Read reviews from multiple sources. If every review says exactly the same thing, be cautious — they might all be affiliate reviews copying the same marketing copy.

6. Avoid fake urgency

"Only 3 left at this price!" "Offer expires in 10 minutes!" These are pressure tactics designed to make you buy before thinking. Legitimate products do not need artificial urgency. Take your time, read the review, and decide calmly.

7. Check for a way to contact the seller

If there is no email, no phone number, no support contact — do not buy. A real company wants to hear from you if something goes wrong. No contact information is a red flag.

What to do if a product does not work

If you buy a product and it does not work for you:

  1. Use it as directed for at least 2-3 weeks — give it a fair chance.
  2. If it still does not work, request a refund through ClickBank at clkbank.com.
  3. Use your order number (from your purchase email) to submit the refund request.
  4. ClickBank processes refunds within 1-5 business days. You do not need to return anything.
Important: You have 60 days from the date of purchase to request a refund. Mark the date when you buy so you do not forget. After 60 days, the guarantee expires.
