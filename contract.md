# Earn-Online — Design & Tone Contract

## Niche
"Earn Extra Online — Real, Boring Systems"
Honest, hype-free affiliate content. Monetized via ClickBank plus direct affiliate programs.

## Tone
- Blunt, direct, patriotic South African.
- No weasel wording: no "some", "most", "fewer than X".
- Use "freedom" not "democracy".
- No SA flag emoji in text. Use 💪 for buttons/footer accents.

## Design
- Real SA flag hero (Wikimedia SVG, inline or linked).
- Green gradients: REJECTED.
- Dark/dimmed UI theme. Muted colours, subtle glows, low-luminance backgrounds.
- No bright/blinding elements.

## Rules
- Never invent numbers. If a product is untested, say so.
- Only list real products with real affiliate links.
- Approval-required products must be flagged honestly.
