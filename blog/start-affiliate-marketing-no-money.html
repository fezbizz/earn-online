How to Start Affiliate Marketing with No Money (2026 Guide)

Posted: August 2026

You want to earn extra online. You have no money to start. This guide shows you exactly how — step by step, using only free tools and free traffic. No paid ads, no courses, no "guru" garbage. Just the boring, honest system that works if you stick with it.

Reality check: This is not a "get rich quick" guide. It takes 3-6 months of consistent effort before you see your first dollar. If you need money this week, get a job. If you want to build a slow, self-sustaining income stream, keep reading.

Step 1: Pick one niche (5 minutes)

Don't overthink this. Pick one topic you can write about for 6 months without getting bored. The best niches for affiliate marketing:

  • Health & Fitness — sleep, weight loss, joint pain, supplements. Evergreen, high commission, massive search volume.
  • Wealth / Make Money — investing, crypto, side hustles. High commission, but more competitive.
  • Self-Help — confidence, relationships, productivity. Steady demand, moderate commission.
  • Hobbies — guitar, gardening, woodworking. Lower commission but passionate audiences and less competition.

Pick ONE. Not three. Not "I'll do health and also crypto and also gardening." One. Niche authority compounds — spreading yourself thin kills it.

Step 2: Get free ClickBank affiliate links (10 minutes)

  1. Go to ClickBank Marketplace and create a free account.
  2. Browse offers in your chosen niche.
  3. Click "Promote" on any offer that doesn't require approval.
  4. Copy your hoplink — that's your affiliate link. It looks like https://yourname.hop.clickbank.net/?vendor=PRODUCT

That's it. You're now an affiliate. You didn't spend a cent. Check the reviews page for offers with real EPC data — that tells you which ones actually convert.

Step 3: Set up a free site or blog (30 minutes)

You need a place to put your affiliate links. Free options:

  • GitHub Pages (what this site uses) — free, custom domain support, full control. Requires basic HTML.
  • Medium.com — free blog platform, built-in audience, but you don't control the platform.
  • Blogger.com — free, Google-owned, easy to use, decent SEO.
  • YouTube — free channel, ranks fast, but requires video creation.

If you want full control (recommended), use GitHub Pages like this site does. If that's too technical, start with Blogger or Medium. The platform matters less than the content.

Step 4: Write your first 10 articles (2-3 weeks)

This is where 90% of people quit. Don't. Write articles that answer questions people are searching for. Each article should be 800-1500 words and answer a specific question honestly.

Article ideas for a health/sleep niche:

  • "Does [product name] actually work?" (product review)
  • "Best sleep aids that aren't melatonin"
  • "Why I can't fall asleep — 5 real reasons"
  • "[product name] vs [competitor] — which is better?"
  • "How long does [product] take to work?"

Put your affiliate link in naturally — when you mention the product, link to it. Don't shove it in. Don't write a 2000-word sales page disguised as a review. Answer the question, link the product, move on.

Step 5: Get free traffic (ongoing, 3-6 months)

This is the hard part. Read the full traffic guide, but here's the short version:

Pick ONE. Do it every day/week for 3 months. Don't add a second method until the first one produces traffic. Focus beats variety every time.

Step 6: First sale and what to do with it

Your first sale will probably take 2-4 months. When it hits:

  1. Don't celebrate yet. One sale is data, not income. Note what content drove the click.
  2. Double down on what worked. If your sleep aid review got the click, write 5 more sleep articles.
  3. Track your numbers. ClickBank shows clicks, hops, and sales. Watch which articles send the most clicks.
  4. Don't expand too early. Stay in one niche until you hit $5/day consistently. Then add a second niche.

The realistic timeline (no promises)

MonthWhat happens
Month 1You write 10-15 articles. Traffic: near zero. No sales. This is normal — don't quit.
Month 2-3Google starts indexing. A few clicks per day. Maybe your first sale. Still feels slow.
Month 4-6Search rankings improve. 20-50 visitors/day. First consistent sales. $1-5/day if you stayed consistent.
Month 6-12If you published 2-3 articles/week, compounding kicks in. $5-20/day is realistic. Not guaranteed.
These are estimates, not promises. Results depend on your niche, content quality, consistency, and luck. I don't guarantee any income. The point is: it takes months, not days. Patience is the strategy.

What NOT to do (the mistakes that kill beginners)

  • Don't pay for ads. You'll lose money. Free traffic first, always.
  • Don't buy courses. Everything you need is free — including this guide.
  • Don't promote 20 products. Pick 2-3 in one niche. Focus.
  • Don't copy-paste vendor sales pages. Write your own honest content. Google penalizes duplicate content.
  • Don't quit in month 2. The graph is flat for months, then it curves up. Quitting in month 2 is the most expensive mistake.

Start now

1. Pick offers in one niche.
2. Pick a traffic method.
3. Write your first article today. Not tomorrow. Today.

The boring system works. The only question is whether you'll stick with it long enough to see it work.
