What makes a good ClickBank offer?

Three things matter, in this order:

  1. EPC (Earnings Per Click): This is the most important number. It tells you how much money affiliates earn per click on average. A high EPC means the offer converts. If EPC is $0 or unreported, the offer is unproven — treat it as a gamble.
  2. Commission: How much you earn per sale. Higher is better, but only if the EPC justifies it. A $200 commission on an offer with $0 EPC is worth less than a $50 commission with $5 EPC.
  3. Gravity: How many affiliates are actively earning. High gravity (100+) means the offer works for many people. Low gravity (under 10) means few affiliates are making money — higher risk, but also less competition.

Should you promote high-commission or high-EPC offers?

High EPC, every time. Here's why:

A $1,700 commission sounds amazing. But if the EPC is $0.10, that means you need 17,000 clicks to make one sale. At a 2% click-through rate, you need 850,000 visitors to make $1,700. That's not realistic for most people.

A $50 commission with $5 EPC means you earn $5 for every 100 visitors. That's $50 per 1,000 visitors — achievable with consistent content. The math works. Start with high-EPC offers, not high-commission ones.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Promoting offers you haven't researched. Check the EPC. If it's $0, you're gambling.
  • Jumping between niches. Pick one niche, stick with it for 6 months. Niche authority compounds.
  • Spamming your link. Forums, comments, DMs — spamming destroys trust and gets you banned.
  • Expecting fast results. Month 1-3 is building. Month 4-6 is when it starts paying. Most people quit in month 2.
  • Ignoring the product quality. If the product is garbage, refunds kill your commissions. Promote things that actually work.
